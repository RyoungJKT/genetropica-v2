What to build next, at the edge of biology

Five genuinely different additions, each styled to match the dashboard and framed for a science-literate reviewer. Every one names what it shows, why it is frontier work, and which existing GeneTropica data it is built from. Tap through, then see the recommended pick.

Direction 01 · Evolutionary medicine

Evolutionary Escape & Durability Predictor

Score every drug-target pairing by how hard it would be for the virus to mutate its way out. A drug that grips highly conserved residues is durable; one that grips variable residues is fragile. Turns the dashboard's conservation data into a forward-looking resistance forecast.

Frontier: anticipating viral evolution, not just binding
Build: high · uses data you already have
Per-residue escape map · DENV NS5 binding site (mock)
Conserved · durable Intermediate Variable · escape-prone
Durability leaderboard · which target resists mutation best (mock)

What it shows

A per-residue escape-risk map and a target durability score = how conserved the residues a drug actually touches are. Flags drugs whose grip sits on mutation-tolerant positions.

Why it is frontier

"Resistance by design" is a live research front: binding-site conservation predicts a high barrier to escape. You forecast viral evolution, the way bnAb and protease-inhibitor work now does.

Built from your data

Already computed: ConSurf grades per residue + chemistry-aware binding contacts per drug. One new derived score, zero fabrication, fully defensible.

Direction 02 · AI structural biology

AI Structure Confidence + Cryptic-Pocket Finder

Overlay AlphaFold-grade per-residue confidence (pLDDT) on each target and surface cryptic pockets the static crystal structure hides. Shows reviewers you know where a model is trustworthy and where new druggable sites may open.

Frontier: AlphaFold 3 / IsoDDE-style pocket discovery
Build: medium · fetch AlphaFold DB + pLDDT
Predicted backbone, colored by model confidence + a flagged cryptic pocket (mock)
Very high pLDDT >90 Confident 70-90 Low 50-70 Disordered <50

What it shows

Confidence-shaded 3D structures so a viewer trusts the well-modeled core and discounts floppy loops, plus transient pockets beyond the crystal.

Why it is frontier

2026's flagship engines find novel pockets from sequence alone. Confidence-aware modeling is now table-stakes for credible structural claims.

Built from your data

Pull AlphaFold DB structures + pLDDT for your six targets; render b-factor coloring in the existing 3Dmol viewer. Pocket flags from a geometric scan.

Direction 03 · Generative chemistry

Generative De Novo Analogue Designer

Take a top hit and let a generative model propose novel analogues that keep the scaffold but improve predicted properties. Each suggestion carries property deltas and an explicit hypothesis-only label, in keeping with the project's honesty rule.

Frontier: diffusion / transformer de novo design
Build: medium · honesty guardrails required
Seed hit → AI-proposed analogues with predicted deltas (mock, hypothetical)

What it shows

Scaffold-preserving novel analogues with predicted affinity / drug-likeness / synthesizability deltas versus the seed, ranked.

Why it is frontier

De novo generative design is the headline of 2026 platforms. It shows you can create chemistry, not just rank an existing library.

Built from your data

Seed from your top hits; generate via a model and re-score with your existing ADMET filters. Caveat: needs strict "hypothetical" framing to protect the honesty canon.

Direction 04 · Network pharmacology

Network Medicine Knowledge Graph

Render the whole screen as an interactive graph of drugs ↔ targets ↔ diseases ↔ pathways, then rank repurposing candidates by network proximity to a disease module. The view experts immediately recognize as systems-level thinking.

Frontier: TxGNN / NetMedGPT zero-shot repurposing
Build: high · graph from existing relations
Drug-target-disease graph · highlighted proximity path (mock)

What it shows

An explorable map of every relationship in the screen, with proximity / centrality scores surfacing polypharmacology hubs and the best-connected repurposing candidates.

Why it is frontier

Network medicine + graph ML is the NTD-repurposing paradigm (most leishmaniasis drugs are repurposed). Foundation models now do this zero-shot.

Built from your data

Your drug-target-disease links already form a graph; add pathway annotations and a force layout. High visual wow, fully grounded.

Direction 05 · Protein language models

Protein-Language-Model Target Atlas

Embed the targets with a protein language model (ESM) to map their relationships, and show an AlphaMissense-style variant-effect heatmap of which mutations the model thinks break each protein. Reframes proteins as something a language model "reads."

Frontier: ESM-2 / AlphaMissense zero-shot effects
Build: medium · precompute embeddings offline
PLM embedding map of targets + variant-effect heatmap (mock)

What it shows

A similarity atlas of the targets and a per-position mutation-tolerance heatmap, predicting which residues are essential without any lab work.

Why it is frontier

Protein LMs are "the new microscope." AlphaMissense-style zero-shot variant calls are a 2024-2026 leap experts will recognize instantly.

Built from your data

Run ESM on the six target sequences offline, ship the embeddings + scores as JSON. Heavier precompute; overlaps with Direction 01's conservation story.

Recommended pick

Direction 01 — Evolutionary Escape & Durability Predictor

It is the only option that turns data you have already computed (conservation grades + binding contacts) into a genuinely new, defensible result with zero fabrication, so it survives a professor's scrutiny. It is squarely "future of biology" (designing drugs against the virus's next move), it deepens the project's existing honesty story instead of risking it, and it ships fast. Network Medicine (04) is the strongest runner-up for pure visual impact and is a natural second build.
